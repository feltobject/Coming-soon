# Coming soon page

Scope: Felt Object's public pre-launch page. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: people curious about playful wool felt products. Job: understand the brand's handmade material world and join the launch waitlist.

Action: submit an email address. Content: the brand name, a wool-felt animal image, and an honest pre-launch message. No catalog, price, social URL, contact email, launch date, testimonials, or product claims have been confirmed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the coming soon page feel like stepping into Felt Object's tiny felt playroom, not a generic countdown page.

OWN-WORLD: Pale ivory is the quiet ground. Saturated orange, electric lime, violet, and pink arrive as bold woolly shapes; the existing orange-and-lime logo cues the palette. Rounded expressive display lettering sits beside a real needle-felt product photograph.

STORY: Visitors meet the brand through a sheep, duck, and bird, play a tiny tap-to-gather game, and can leave their email to hear when the shop opens. Signup data is stored in Cloudflare KV after the binding is configured; there is no automatic email service yet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The small stacked wordmark and opening status sit in a fine top rail. Oversized three-line headline and one email-list action own the left half. The right half is a large tactile animal scene on the pale background, accented by floating geometric shapes. Three wool balls below it invite a quick tap interaction.

FORM: Felt playroom / mini craft-world, candidate 3. seed:7d3a8de163201d5f.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
