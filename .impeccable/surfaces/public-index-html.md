# Coming soon page

Scope: Felt Object's public pre-launch page. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: people curious about playful wool felt products. Job: understand the brand's handmade material world and join the launch waitlist.

Action: submit an email address. Content: the brand name, a wool-felt animal image, and an honest pre-launch message. No catalog, price, social URL, contact email, launch date, testimonials, or product claims have been confirmed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the coming soon page feel like stepping into Felt Object's tiny felt playroom, not a generic countdown page.

OWN-WORLD: A pale sunny-yellow ground and textile-rich felt collage carry the tiny craft-playroom world. Use the exact bold bright orange and neon lime from the supplied logo, with no muddy orange or off-palette green. Rounded expressive display lettering holds the copy; cut felt layers, visible fibers, and stitched edges carry the animal scene.

STORY: Visitors meet the brand through a sheep, duck, and bird in a layered felt setting, toss five colored wool puffs, watch the flock wobble, and can leave their email to hear when the shop opens. Signup data is stored in Cloudflare KV after the binding is configured; there is no automatic email service yet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The transparent stacked logo from the supplied image and an opening status sit in a fine top rail. An oversized three-line headline and one lime email-list action own the left half. The right half is a full-bleed sunshine-yellow felt-collage panel with stitched sheep, duck, and bird forms. Its bright lime game strip offers three tappable puffs; each toss drops a colored ball and makes the flock wiggle. On mobile the headline condenses to two lines and the full game fits beneath the scene.
