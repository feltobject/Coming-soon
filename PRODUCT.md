# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML/CSS with a small Cloudflare Pages Function for waitlist submissions; Cloudflare Pages is the intended host.

## Users

People interested in playful wool felt goods, including animal figures and small handmade accessories.

## Product Purpose

Felt Object is a new brand preparing to sell wool felt and felting-related products. The coming soon page introduces the brand and lets interested visitors join an email waitlist.

## Positioning

Open decision: specific product range and distinctive brand position are still being developed.

## Operating Context

The coming soon page will be published through a GitHub repository connected to Cloudflare Pages and the brand's domain.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Email waitlist signups should be stored by the Cloudflare-hosted page.
- No email marketing provider or notification workflow has been selected yet.
- Exact domain, social profile URLs, launch date, and customer contact address remain open.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Felt Object.
- The brand is for wool felt and felting-related products.
- The user prefers playful, funky, high-energy colors, especially orange and electric yellow/lime.
- Match the bright orange and neon-lime colors from the supplied Felt Object logo; avoid muddy orange and alternate green shades.
- The user prefers a white or off-white background and realistic felt texture.
- The existing logo uses bold rounded orange lowercase lettering, stacked, with a lime underline on pale cream.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing logo images are in the project root.
- No confirmed product catalog, product photography, launch date, testimonials, or sales claims yet.

## Product Principles

- Make the material and products feel tactile and joyful.
- Keep the brand's playful visual character recognizable.
- Do not imply products, dates, or capabilities that are not confirmed.
